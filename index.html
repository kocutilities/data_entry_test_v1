KOC Data Center

Electrical records for Data Center No. 1 — load readings now, system assessment and additional-load studies to follow.

Load Reading

R, Y and B phase currents for 26 incomers and boards, plus every outgoing breaker on PDU 1 to PDU 8. Saves to a Google Sheet.

Single Line Diagram

Transformers, generators, ATSs, the LT board and both UPS chains down to the cabins — with the current flow animated from the readings recorded for a chosen date.

Power System Assessment

The recorded currents judged against the KOC criteria — transformer contingency capacity, generator capacity, feeder ratings and the headroom left for additional load. Every verdict cites its clause.

Additional Load Study

A proposed load run through the KOC assessment sequence — transformer contingency, generator, every feeder on the supply path, and the UPS redundancy. Accept or reject, with the clause and the margin.
